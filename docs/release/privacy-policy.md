# Privacy policy (draft, needs owner and legal review)

VerbNest does not collect, send or sell personal data. It has no accounts, analytics or advertising.

- **What the app stores:** your workspaces (requests, variables, history, cookies, certificates you add) as files on your device. Secrets and proxy passwords use the operating system's secret store when it is available; if it is not, the app says so in Settings and stores them unencrypted on the device.
- **What the app sends:** only the HTTP requests you write and send, to the servers you choose, and OAuth sign-in requests you start. The developer never receives them.
- **Sharing:** exports (workspace file, Postman JSON, YAML snapshot) are written only where you choose. They contain no secrets.
- **Deleting data:** delete a workspace or uninstall the app.
- **Contact:** to be set by the owner.
