# Code of Conduct

We want VerbNest's issues, pull requests and discussions to be a place where people can do good work together.

## What we expect
- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive. Say what you saw, what you expected and why.
- Welcome newcomers; explain instead of dismissing.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and boundaries. Do not share anyone's personal information.

## What is not acceptable
- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination or demeaning comments about a person or group.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, spam and promotion unrelated to the project.
- Publishing private information, or other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules, with or without warning depending on how serious it is. This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

To report a problem, email **info@omnits.pro**. Reports are kept confidential and handled by the maintainers; if a report concerns a maintainer, say so and it will be handled by another one.
