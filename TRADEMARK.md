## If you fork or redistribute a modified version
1. Give it a **different name and a different icon**.
2. Keep the copyright and licence notices (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE`) as the Apache licence requires, and state what you changed.
3. You may say "based on VerbNest" or "a fork of VerbNest" in plain text, as long as it is clear yours is a separate project not made or endorsed by us.
